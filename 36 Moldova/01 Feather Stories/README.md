Misreads stay inside six look-alike groups (AKRX, BGSZ, CDOQ, EFLP, HMNW, IJTY). Each site swaps letters in pairs inside a group. On held-out reads, site explains the errors better than observer, year, district or observer×site.
24 White Stork reads match no registered ring. All are at one Lower Prut site (L5819951, Cahul), 2016–2025, by 24 observers. Their letter groups rule out every registered ring, and they form one bird.
What observers see there is RHGLWQ (R 19/23, H 19/22, G 13/16, L 15/19, W 18/22, Q 17/19). The platform rejected that as the ring, so the site swaps letters.
Nothing registered is read at that site, so the swap is inferred. Both halves of its letters carry ~15% noise, which only happens where a swap is active. The most common two-half swap at calibrated sites (b+e, 9 clean sites) decodes RHGLWQ to XWBPHD.
